### (b) GAP-BA-001 — livestock `price_or_terms` vs schema

- `[CRITICAL][contract][CONFIRMED] docs/02-requirements/business-requirements/livestock-marketplace.md:26-29,178,194-199 (+pet-marketplace.md:25,170,187-192) → BR mandates a free-text `price_or_terms` field carrying "negotiable", "50000 per straw", "package: 3 straws + synchronization", "pick of litter"; `database_schema.sql:252` has ONLY `price_cents BIGINT` (+`quantity INTEGER`, `currency CHAR(3)`) — no `price_terms_text`/`price_or_terms` column anywhere. `listing.dto.ts:102,165,372` and `listing.service.ts` (grep `priceCents`) confirm the API is integer-cents-only. Fix: either add `price_terms_text VARCHAR(150)` alongside `price_cents` (additive, no rewrite) and update the DTO/validation to accept either, or formally amend the BR to price_cents-only and strip the free-text examples (with the WHAT/WHY/WHY-BETTER triple, since it changes seller-facing capability).`

### (c) Ownership-transfer counterparty is unfindable by a normal user

- `[BLOCKER][dropped-need][NEW] backend/src/modules/animal/dto/transfer.dto.ts:12-21 (TransferInitiateDto) + docs/specs/02-animal-domain.md:125,168,184 → `POST /animals/{id}/transfers` requires `toUserId` (a UUID) or `toOrganizationId` (a UUID) supplied by the initiator up front. No endpoint anywhere in the codebase lets a normal (non-ADMIN) user resolve a human they know in real life (by phone/email/username) to a `userId`: `admin-user.controller.ts` is `@Roles('ADMIN')`-gated (grant-role/rebind/erase only, not a lookup); grep for `users/search`/`user-search`/`lookup` across backend/src returns no non-admin user-search or user-by-phone/email endpoint. The BR narrative ("the current owner initiates a transfer to the recipient") and the Gherkin (`animal-domain.md:184`: "POSTs /animals/{id}/transfers with toUserId = R") both silently assume the initiator already possesses R's UUID — a datum no human interface ever hands them. In practice: a breeder selling a stud to another breeder they've met via a listing has no in-product way to name them as a transfer recipient. → add a scoped user-lookup (e.g. by verified phone/handle, rate-limited, minimal-PII response — full_name + userId only) OR let the transfer be seeded from an existing counterparty context (e.g. "transfer to the buyer who revealed contact on listing X" — reuses `contact_reveals.viewer_id`, which the system already has and is a much smaller privacy surface than an open user-search).`
