# Terms of Service / Platform Rules — ZooLink   (STATUS: DRAFT)

> **DRAFT for owner review.** Incorporated by reference into the Public Offer (`public-offer.md`).
> RU version in `docsRU/legal/` is operative. Basis: ФЗ-149, ГК ст.1253.1, 498-ФЗ, ЗоЗПП,
> 61-ФЗ (vet drugs), CITES / ст.258.1 УК РФ, ФЗ-38 (advertising). Analysis dated 2026-06-30.

## 1. Scope
These Rules govern conduct on the ZooLink Platform and are an integral part of the Agreement (the Public Offer). By using the Platform the User accepts these Rules. They apply to **both markets** (pet and livestock, ADR-0002); market-specific clauses are marked.

## 2. Account
2.1. Registration requires a valid phone number (SMS-verified) and/or OAuth (Google/Apple/Telegram/VK). One person — one account; accounts are non-transferable.
2.2. The User is responsible for activity under the account and for keeping credentials secure.
2.3. Minors and persons without legal capacity may not transact; the Platform is intended for capable Users.

## 3. Listings — general rules
3.1. A listing must be accurate, relate to a real animal the Seller is entitled to offer, and fall in the correct market and category.
3.2. Prohibited in any listing: false information; stock/unrelated photos; another's contacts; spam/duplicate listings; offers of prohibited goods (§5); content infringing third-party rights.
3.3. Listings are **pre-moderated** (ADR-0003) and become visible only after approval. The Operator may approve, request changes, or reject with a reason.
3.4. Exact address/GPS is never published; only approximate location/distance is shown (ADR-0005).

## 4. Animal-welfare and trade rules (498-ФЗ)
4.1. Listings must comply with **498-ФЗ «Об ответственном обращении с животными»**: no content promoting cruelty, baiting, illegal fighting, or treatment violating welfare requirements.
4.2. **Pet market:** sale/transfer of companion animals must respect 498-ФЗ; the Platform encourages responsible rehoming. Adoption/`adoption`-type listings are non-commercial.
4.3. **Livestock market:** Sellers are responsible for compliance with veterinary requirements. Animals/products subject to state veterinary control require electronic veterinary certificates via **ФГИС ВетИС («Меркурий»)**; **in the MVP the Platform does not issue or verify vet certificates** — the Seller obtains and the Buyer verifies them off-platform (vet-cert integration is a later phase). The Platform is an information venue only.

## 5. Prohibited and restricted goods/species
5.1. **Prohibited (listing forbidden, removed on sight, may be reported to authorities):**
   - Species listed in CITES / the Red Book of the RF and species whose trade is restricted by law; trafficking in specially protected species is a crime (**ст.258.1 УК РФ**).
   - Animals/items whose circulation is restricted or banned in the RF.
   - **Medicinal products, including veterinary prescription drugs** (61-ФЗ; sale of medicines requires a pharmaceutical licence — out of scope; the `goods`/pharmacy track keeps Rx **OFF**).
5.2. **Restricted (allowed only with the required documents / age-gating):**
   - Breeding/pedigree animals — claims of pedigree must be backed by documents on request.
   - Any category requiring permits — the Seller must hold them.
5.3. The Operator maintains a moderation reason `prohibited_species` and removes violating listings; repeat violations lead to account sanctions.

## 6. Contact exchange (no chat in MVP — ADR-0005)
6.1. After a listing is ACTIVE, an authenticated Buyer may reveal the Seller's chosen contacts (phone / Telegram / VK), within rate limits (anti-harvesting). Exact address is never revealed.
6.2. Publication/revealing of the Seller's contacts is a **distribution of personal data** and requires the Seller's separate consent на распространение (ст.10.1 ФЗ-152) — `consent-personal-data.md`. The Seller controls which contact methods are shown and may withdraw at any time.
6.3. Contacts may be used only to discuss the specific listing; harvesting, spam, and resale of contacts are prohibited.

## 7. User-generated content licence
7.1. The User warrants they own or are licensed to use all uploaded Content (photos, text) and that it does not infringe third-party IP or personal-data rights of others.
7.2. The User grants the Operator a non-exclusive, royalty-free, worldwide licence to host, display, cache, resize, index and moderate the Content for operating and promoting the Platform, for the publication term and a reasonable period thereafter for backups/legal hold.
7.3. **Takedown:** rights holders may submit a complaint (ФЗ-149 / ГК ст.1253.1 procedure) to `[abuse contact]`; the Operator reviews and removes infringing Content on a valid claim.

## 8. Moderation, complaints and appeals
8.1. The Operator pre-moderates listings and may remove Content or restrict accounts violating these Rules or the law (lawful content control, ФЗ-149 ст.10).
8.2. **Grounds** for removal/sanction: any breach of §§3–7, fraud, abuse, illegal content, repeated violations.
8.3. **Appeal:** a User may contest a moderation decision via `[appeal channel]`; the appeal is reviewed by a human (human override of automated decisions — §9). The Operator aims to respond within the SLA published in the product.

## 9. Automated decisions (AI operators — ADR-0006)
9.1. Some operational and moderation decisions may be made by **automated systems / AI agents** acting for the Operator. The User is **informed** that a decision may be automated.
9.2. The User has the right to request **human review** of an automated decision affecting them (appeal, §8.3). Accountability for automated decisions remains with the Operator.

## 10. Sanctions
Warning → content removal → listing/account suspension → account termination, proportionate to the violation. Fraud, illegal-trade and welfare-crime content may be reported to the authorities.

## 11. Consumer rights (ЗоЗПП)
For the Operator's *own* paid services (when introduced), individual consumers retain their ЗоЗПП rights. These Rules do not make the Operator a party to, or guarantor of, transactions **between** Users.

## 12. Changes
The Operator may amend these Rules; the current version is published on the Platform with its effective date. Continued use after the effective date is acceptance.
