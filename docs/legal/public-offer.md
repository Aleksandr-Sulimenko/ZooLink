## 1. General provisions
1.1. This document is a **public offer** (ст.435, 437 ГК РФ) of `[Operator: ИП/ООО «…», ОГРН/ОГРНИП …, ИНН …, address …]` (the **"Operator"**) addressed to any capable individual or legal entity (the **"User"**) to conclude an agreement to use the ZooLink platform (the **"Platform"**) on the terms below.
1.2. **Acceptance** of the offer (ст.438 ГК РФ) is the User's completion of registration and/or any use of the Platform. Acceptance creates a binding agreement between the User and the Operator (the **"Agreement"**) and signifies full and unconditional acceptance of this offer, the **Terms of Service / Rules** (`terms-of-service.md`) and the **Privacy Policy** (`privacy-policy.md`), which are integral parts of the Agreement.
1.3. The Platform is an online service for placing and searching listings about animals, hard-separated into two markets (ADR-0002): **pet** (companion animals) and **livestock** (agricultural/breeding animals). The two markets carry different rules; see the Rules.

## 2. Definitions
- **Platform** — the ZooLink website and its services at `[domain]`.
- **User** — a registered visitor (Seller and/or Buyer).
- **Seller** — a User who publishes a listing.
- **Buyer** — a User who searches/views listings and may request the Seller's contacts.
- **Listing** — User-generated content offering an animal for sale, breeding/mating, adoption, exhibition, etc.
- **Content** — any information a User uploads (text, photos, contacts).

## 3. Subject of the Agreement
3.1. The Operator grants the User access to the Platform's functionality: registration, profile and animal management, creation and search of listings, and — after moderation — exchange of contact information for off-platform communication (in-app chat is not provided in the MVP — ADR-0005).
3.2. In the MVP the basic Platform services are provided **free of charge**. Paid services (promotion, premium, transactions) may be introduced later; they are governed by separate terms and are inactive until enabled (`feature_toggles`). The Operator does **not** receive payment for deals between Users and does **not** process such payments in the MVP.

## 4. Status of the Operator — information intermediary
4.1. The Operator acts as an **information intermediary** (ст.1253.1 ГК РФ) and an **information-dissemination organiser of an information system** providing a venue for Users' listings. The Operator is **not a party** to any sale, mating, adoption or other transaction between Users, does not own the animals, does not guarantee their characteristics, health, pedigree, or the accuracy of Users' Content.
4.2. All transactions between Users are concluded **directly between them**, at their own risk and responsibility. Disputes between Users are resolved between them; the Operator is not an arbiter and bears no liability for the parties' obligations.
4.3. The Operator performs **pre-moderation** of listings (ADR-0003) to enforce the Rules and the law, and may remove Content or restrict access under the conditions in the Rules. Moderation is a lawful-content-control measure (ФЗ-149 ст.10), not an assumption of liability for Content.
4.4. Some moderation and operational decisions may be performed by **automated/AI agents** acting on the Operator's behalf (ADR-0006); such decisions remain the Operator's responsibility, are subject to human override on appeal, and are disclosed to the User (see Rules §"Automated decisions").

## 5. Rights and obligations
5.1. The User undertakes to: provide accurate data; comply with the Rules and RF law; not publish prohibited or restricted goods/species (see Rules); hold the rights to all Content uploaded; not use the Platform for fraud, harassment, or scraping.
5.2. The User grants the Operator a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to use uploaded Content **solely** to operate and promote the Platform (display, cache, resize, index, moderate) for the term of publication; details in the Rules.
5.3. The Operator may: moderate, suspend or remove listings and accounts that violate the Rules or law; change Platform functionality; suspend the Platform for maintenance.
5.4. The Operator does not guarantee uninterrupted or error-free operation and provides the Platform on an **"as is"** basis to the extent permitted by law.

## 6. Personal data
6.1. Processing of the User's personal data is governed by the **Privacy Policy** (`privacy-policy.md`) and ФЗ-152. The lawful basis for processing data necessary to perform this Agreement is **ст.6 ч.1 п.5 ФЗ-152** (performance of a contract to which the data subject is a party). Marketing, analytics and the publication (distribution) of the Seller's contacts in listings are based on **separate consents** (ст.9, ст.10.1 ФЗ-152) — see `consent-personal-data.md`.

## 7. Liability
7.1. To the maximum extent permitted by RF law, the Operator is **not liable** for: the actions of Users; the accuracy/legality of Users' Content; the outcome, quality or safety of transactions between Users; the animals' health/characteristics; off-platform communications and losses arising from them.
7.2. The Operator's liability, where it cannot be excluded, is limited to direct documented damage. The Operator is not liable for lost profit.
7.3. Nothing in this offer limits the rights that consumers (individuals acquiring for personal needs) have under **ЗоЗПП** in relation to the Operator's *own* paid services (when introduced); it does **not** make the Operator a party to User-to-User deals.

## 8. Term, modification, termination
8.1. The Agreement takes effect on acceptance and is open-ended.
8.2. The Operator may amend this offer and the Rules; the current version is published on the Platform. Material changes are notified to Users. Continued use after a change is acceptance of the new version; a User who disagrees may stop using the Platform and delete the account.
8.3. The User may terminate at any time by deleting the account (right to erasure — `privacy-policy.md`). The Operator may terminate/suspend access for Rules/law violations.

## 9. Governing law and disputes
9.1. The Agreement is governed by the law of the **Russian Federation**.
9.2. Disputes are resolved through mandatory pre-trial claim procedure (response term 30 days); failing settlement — in the court at the Operator's location, subject to mandatory consumer-jurisdiction rules of ЗоЗПП where applicable.
