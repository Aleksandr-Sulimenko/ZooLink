**Scope:** full cross-functional audit of the implemented system (code + docs + specs) **(Part A)** and of the
"Ecosystem Expansion" vision `docsRU/01-discovery/future-features.md:145-227` **(Part B)**.
**Method:** 16 specialist sub-agents (architect, alpha-analyst, backend-engineer, reviewer-qa, security, legal,
